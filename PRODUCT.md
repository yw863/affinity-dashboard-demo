# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step and no framework. A single self-contained `index.html` (inline CSS and JS, hand-drawn SVG, no third-party chart library) plus separate data files. Everything must run from `file://` by double-click on a machine with no toolchain and possibly no network, so data ships as classic scripts attached to `window.AFFINITY_DATA` rather than ES modules. The deliverable is a zip (`.js` attachments get stripped by mail filters).

## Users

Two audiences, held at once:

- **Stated user — the sensory science team** (L'Oréal R&I, New Jersey hub). Scientists who will use it day to day to pull AI sensory readings for a formula, track it across wear time, and compare it against a benchmark formula. They draw sensory profiles vertically and export charts into slide decks.
- **Real audience — leadership.** The tool is demonstrated live by its author to senior, non-technical leaders. It must look like a credible working tool and impress on sight. The viewer is assumed to understand nothing that the controls do not make obvious.

## Product Purpose

A toolbox for AI sensory readings of foundation. A DINOv2-based model reads Optiscreen photographs and returns scores on the eight sensory attributes, replacing the human panel. Users import a formula's AI readings (an Excel file of precomputed scores), then:

- **Dynamic** — one formula, its readings across T0 / T3 / T6, with significant changes against the baseline time marked;
- **Comparison** — one bench formula plus up to two others, across all three time points, with significant differences against the bench marked as better or worse.

Success: the leader watches an import turn into a chart in two clicks, sees at a glance where formulas differ and whether that is good or bad, and leaves believing the tool is ready for the team. Failure modes: looking generic or machine-made, needing explanation, or feeling like a static mock.

## Positioning

The AI readings stand on their own; the panel is no longer shown. The only trace of validation is a quiet model badge (83.4% acceptance against the panel, mock copy) with a hover card. The screen's argument is **"this is a tool the team can work with"**, not "the AI agrees with the panel".

## Operating Context

- Built on macOS; **presented from a Windows work ThinkPad (small ~13–14" screen)**, possibly offline and behind a corporate network. Rendering and pointer precision must be judged there.
- Single fixed canvas 1920 × 1050 scaled to fit, no page scroll (the library list scrolls internally).
- Live talk-through by the author; hidden keyboard shortcuts support rehearsal and reset.
- Fonts and every asset are self-hosted (OFL `.woff2`, embedded as data URIs); no runtime CDN.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Domain model: formulas × 3 wear time points (T0 / T3 / T6) × 8 fixed sensory attributes, AI score only, on a 0–6 scale to one decimal.
- The 8 attributes and the 0–6 scale are fixed by the sensory team.
- **Significance:** a difference counts as significant when `|x − base| ≥ threshold` for that attribute (per-attribute thresholds, currently mock).
- **Polarity:** COVERAGE and REFLECTION vs. BARE are better when higher; the other six are better when lower.
- Formulas are identified by their formula ID only (e.g. `b53176_NARS #L2`). There is no ours / market distinction.
- **Faked interfaces:** file import (any file is accepted; contents are not read — the demo reveals the pre-loaded `Lancome TIU #PO_01_Anker 4.15`), model card details, record metadata, template download.
- **Real interfaces:** selection logic, significance computation, PNG export, copy-to-clipboard, CSV export of readings.
- Data is **mock**, generated by `scripts/generate-data.mjs` (seeded): three formulas with real IDs plus 20 labelled placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Title: **Foundation Affinity × AI-lization**. Subtitle: **AI readings powered by DINOv2**.
- The desaturated brand purple is the company's presentation colour and stays the page's identity.
- Aesthetic: fashion / beauty editorial — Morandi palette, Didone display type, hairlines, generous space — and **rich, not muted**. Motion, ornament and small considered details are encouraged.
- **No AI slop**, and its counterweight: **under-design is an equal failure**. Avoid unnecessary card containers; overlays may use refined glass.
- `DESIGN.md` is the authoritative visual and interaction specification. It governs.

## Evidence on Hand

- `DESIGN.md` — the v2 specification.
- `data-*.js` — mock AI readings (to be regenerated for the v2 schema).
- `scripts/generate-data.mjs` — the generator.
- `Screenshot 2026-09-18 at 00.52.36.png` — a real Lancome TIU panel chart, the trend reference for the mock data.
- `specimens/` — typeface comparison pages the user chose from (Libre Bodoni + Red Hat Text).
- **Absent, must not be fabricated:** real thresholds, real model card figures, real data for any formula beyond the three named, product or Optiscreen imagery.

## Product Principles

1. **A tool first.** Every element answers a working question about formulas; nothing exists only to make a claim.
2. **Fool-proof by structure.** Rules live in the shape of the controls, not in help text.
3. **One mode, one chart.** Dynamic and Comparison never share a chart.
4. **Colour means something.** Series colour identifies; a separate pair marks better / worse; the baseline has none.
5. **Nothing invented.** Placeholders are labelled as placeholders.
6. **Windows-offline or it does not exist.**
7. **Fancy and considered.** Distinctive richness, never generic, never bare.
