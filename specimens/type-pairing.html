Type Pairing

Part 1 holds the sans constant (Hanken Grotesk) and compares the display serif on the stat tiles. Part 2 holds the serif constant (Libre Bodoni) and compares the sans on labels, axis text and tooltip numbers. Every face here is OFL on Google Fonts, so it can be self-hosted and renders the same on Windows. The tnum tag is measured live in this browser: it checks that all digits have equal width with tabular-nums on.
