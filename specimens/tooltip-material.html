Tooltip Material

Five materials under the same tooltip, each shown twice: over a dense patch of chart (where blur has something to work with) and over empty paper below a stat tile (where it does not). The second bed is the one that decides it — a translucent light layer on #F5F3F7 has almost no luminance to trade on, so whatever replaces the hairline has to hold the edge there.

Watch the 12px labels, not the plate.
Pick the bed, not the plate. A material only exists in relation to what is behind it. Judge each row by the right-hand bed — if the tooltip stops being a tooltip over blank paper, the material is wrong no matter how well it reads over the lines.
